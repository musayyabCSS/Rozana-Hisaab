1. Overview

Rozana Hisaab is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how your data is collected, used, and safeguarded when using our smart ledger and expense tracking web application.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect the information necessary to provide account services and record keeping:

  • Account Information: Name and email address provided during authentication (via Google OAuth or email sign-in).
  • Financial Records: Transaction entries, amounts, vendors, categories, and timestamps entered by you.
  • AI Input Data: Audio clips recorded for voice expense logging and receipt photos captured for OCR parsing are processed securely in real-time via server-side Edge Functions.

3. How Your Information Is Used

Your data is used strictly to provide, synchronize, and improve your personal ledger experience. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal or financial data to any third parties or advertisers.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encrypted PostgreSQL databases via Supabase, protected by Row Level Security (RLS) ensuring that only your authenticated account can access your records.

5. Data Deletion & Inquiries

You may request permanent deletion of your account and associated transaction history at any time. For privacy inquiries or deletion requests, please Contact Support.
