When you choose to speak or scan a receipt, that voice clip or photo is sent through our server to OpenAI to extract the text. We do not store it. OpenAI processes it under its own API data terms. Your voice recordings and receipt photos stay on your device only.

The text details of each entry (title, amount, category, vendor, items, bill and account numbers) are backed up to your account.

You can delete your data from Settings and delete media using Delete my media.
