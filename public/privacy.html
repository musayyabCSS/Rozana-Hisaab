Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Welcome to Rozana Hisaab ("we", "our", or "us"). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Rozana Hisaab, we collect:

  • Account Details: Basic profile data (such as your email address and name) provided via Google Sign-In or manual registration.
  • Financial Entries: Transaction amounts, merchant descriptions, dates, categories, and payment statuses that you enter.
  • Receipt Images: Images of receipts and bills that you upload for automated optical character recognition (OCR).

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to:

  • Provide, maintain, and personalize your expense tracking ledger.
  • Authenticate your identity securely through Supabase.
  • Extract merchant and total values from uploaded receipt images via AI processing.

3. Data Protection and Access

Your transactions and user profile are isolated and protected by PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS). We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial data to any third party.

4. Third-Party Services

We work with trusted infrastructure providers to power application features:

  • Google Identity Services: Account authentication.
  • Supabase: Cloud database storage and session management.
  • OpenAI: Automated receipt scanning and parsing.

5. Contact & Deletion Requests

You can request data deletion or ask questions regarding this policy by contacting us at: musayyab@chatsupportservice.com.
